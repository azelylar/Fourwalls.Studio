Get in Touch

Visit or reach out to FOURWALLS.STUDIO

A warm, natural-light studio in the heart of the arts district — built for movement, sound, and story.

📍 Map preview — 21 Woodland Close, #06-34

FOURWALLS.STUDIO is a 2,400 sq ft creative space split across dance, podcast, event, classroom, photography and videography configurations. Originally a 1960s shophouse warehouse, we've kept the exposed beams and polished concrete while adding professional-grade sound treatment, lighting rigs, and a sprung dance floor. Our mission is to give independent creators and small teams access to studio-grade space without a studio-grade price tag.
